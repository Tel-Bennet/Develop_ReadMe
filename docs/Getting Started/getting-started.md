Use Telegent mPaaS, a Mobile Platform as a Service API, to build and manage mobile services without owning carrier infrastructure. Provision phone numbers, send messages, and route calls through a single REST API.

</Cards>

## What you can build

<Cards>
  <Card kind="tile" title="Phone Number Provisioning" href="/reference/numbers" icon="fa-duotone fa-phone">Provision and manage mobile virtual network operator (MVNO) and Internet of Things (IoT) numbers by area code or zip code.</Card>

  <Card kind="tile" title="SMS & MMS Messaging" href="/reference/message" icon="fa-duotone fa-message">Send and receive application-to-person (A2P) and person-to-person (P2P) messages with delivery callbacks.</Card>

  <Card kind="tile" title="Voice Routing" href="/reference/voice" icon="fa-duotone fa-phone-volume">Configure Session Initiation Protocol (SIP) trunks, interactive voice response (IVR) menus, workgroups, and call routing rules.</Card>

  <Card kind="tile" title="Number Porting" href="/reference/numbers" icon="fa-duotone fa-arrows-left-right">Port numbers in and out of Telegent from other carriers.</Card>

  <Card kind="tile" title="Subscriber Management" href="/reference/subscribers" icon="fa-duotone fa-users">Create end-user accounts and assign numbers, plans, and permissions.</Card>

  <Card kind="tile" title="AI Guardian" href="/reference/subscribers" icon="fa-duotone fa-shield">Apply content filtering, parental controls, and call and message blocking per subscriber.</Card>
</Cards>
