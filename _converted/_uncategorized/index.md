Telegent mPaaS is a Mobile Platform as a Service API that lets you build and manage mobile services — from provisioning phone numbers to sending messages and routing calls — without owning carrier infrastructure.

- **[Get Started — Partner Onboarding](/onboarding)** — Follow the Partner Onboarding plan to go from POC kickoff to a live, production-ready integration.

---

## What you can build

  - **[Phone Number Provisioning](/api-reference/numbers/search-available-numbers)** — Provision and manage MVNO and IoT numbers by area code or zip code

  - **[SMS & MMS Messaging](/api-reference/message/create-message-route)** — Send and receive A2P and P2P messages with delivery callbacks

  - **[Voice Routing](/api-reference/voice/create-voice-route)** — Configure SIP trunks, IVR menus, workgroups, and call routing rules

  - **[Number Porting](/api-reference/numbers/numbers-port-in)** — Port numbers in and out of Telegent from other carriers

  - **[Subscriber Management](/api-reference/subscribers/get-subscriber)** — Create end-user accounts and assign numbers, plans, and permissions

  - **[AI Guardian](/api-reference/ai-guardian/create-message-filter)** — Apply content filtering, parental controls, and call/message blocking per subscriber
